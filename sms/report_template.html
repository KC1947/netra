Evidence coverage caveat

Risk and coverage are separate. Missing, encrypted, or otherwise unobservable evidence lowers evidence coverage; it never lowers observed risk or counts as proof of safety.

This passive report makes no active DNS, OCSP, CRL, or server queries. Conclusions are limited to captured bytes. DEDUCED facts follow deterministic evidence-preserving logic; INFERRED facts do not become observed inventory.
