# M7 calibration correction — 27 September 2026

The production model now disables degenerate scales, excludes constant
reference features, calibrates on disjoint captures, and cites only genuinely
rare reference bins. Findings, risk, coverage, HNDL and all other non-ML report
fields remain unchanged.

## Diagnosis confirmed before editing

The previous production baseline was `out/baseline.pcap`, generated by
`lab/generate_corpus.py`: 240 synthetic sessions, split by row into 192 training
and 48 calibration sessions. Both calibration MADs were zero and replaced by
`1e-9`. The calibration maximum was 3,031,327,477.8549867; real-mail scores ranged
from 7,967,967,385.581675 to 42,333,170,935.428. All 37 real-mail sessions were
flagged and every percentile was 100.0. Factor selection unconditionally took
three entries, including implicit TLS at 53.122754% and certificate visibility
at 69.788553% in the two requested reports.

## Baseline and decision semantics

`ml/baseline.json` contains 215 feature rows from six captures:

| Source | Source sessions | Baseline sessions |
|---|---:|---:|
| `real_mail.pcap` | 37 | 37 |
| `mixed_enterprise.pcap` | 25 | 18 |
| `benign_101.pcap` through `benign_104.pcap` | 160 | 160 |

The recorded traffic is assumed benign, not independently attack-labelled.
Enterprise excludes the seven planted security-risk cases listed below.
Generated captures vary protocol/upgrade mode, TLS version and offers, RSA/EC
certificates, certificate age at capture, fingerprint shares, and connection
length. Their packet timestamps are ordered. The checked-in artifact retains
only numeric features, source hashes, and session IDs; NaN is stored as null.

The target capture's SHA-256 is excluded before fitting. Renamed exact copies
remain excluded. GroupKFold(3) keeps every original capture together. Source
priorities choose the calibration fold: curated enterprise normals first,
assumed-benign recorded traffic second, wholly generated traffic last; ties
follow GroupKFold order. Priorities do not use evaluation scores. Remaining
folds train the estimators. This is one fixed, source-selected holdout per
target, not an average across all folds.

| Scoring capture | Training sessions | Calibration sessions | Calibration captures |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| New/unseen capture | 157 | 58 | benign_103 + enterprise normals |
| real_mail | 120 | 58 | benign_103 + enterprise normals |
| mixed_enterprise | 120 | 77 | benign_103 + real_mail |

The new component scales (`1.4826 * MAD`) are positive: for real-mail scoring,
IF 0.03556517 and HBOS 1.82104097; for enterprise scoring, IF 0.02702568 and HBOS
3.03297546. A zero scale disables its component, with no epsilon substitute.
Constant or unavailable reference columns contribute exactly zero in both
estimators, including when the scoring value is outside the constant's range.
If neither component can score, the session explicitly abstains with null
score/percentile and no flag. Partial component abstention is separately visible
in calibration provenance.

HBOS histogram resolution is bounded by the Freedman–Diaconis width, from two
to ten bins for varying columns. This avoids treating routine interpolation in
sparse columns as a large signal. Unseen-value smoothing uses the same
population denominator as in-range bins. Rarity explanations use unsmoothed
observed bin frequencies: **at most 5%**, with at most three factors and no
padding. A disabled feature/component is never cited. Empty factor lists carry
an explicit explanation that no single factor clears the cutoff. The decision
threshold remains immediately above the calibration 99th percentile.
